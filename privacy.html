Privacy policy

Privacy, plainly.

Screwhaus works without an account. Your game lives on your phone, and play data is shared only if you say yes.

Kept on your iPhone

  • Your levels, coins, boosters, lives, streak and daily rewards.
  • Your choices, such as whether to share play data and get reminders.
  • Feedback you type into the in-game feedback form. In this version it stays on your device and is not sent to us.

The home-screen widget reads the same on-device data. Reminders such as "lives are full" are scheduled on your phone; no server sends them.

Shared only if you agree

After your first win, the game asks whether you want to share how you play. If you say yes, it sends:

  • Gameplay events: levels started, won and lost, how long they took, boosters used, and which offers you saw or bought.
  • Screen recordings of play, so we can see where a puzzle gets confusing. Text you type is hidden from them.
  • Basic device details: iPhone model, iOS version, app version, language and country.
  • A random install ID created by the app, so events from one install can be grouped. It isn't your name, email, Apple Account or advertising identifier.

This data is processed by PostHog, our analytics provider, on servers in the United States. We use it only to improve the game: to fix puzzles that are too hard, find bugs and tune difficulty. It isn't linked to your identity, isn't used for advertising, and isn't sold.

If you say no, nothing is sent. You can change your answer at any time in the shop under Privacy.

Purchases

Apple handles payments. We never see your card or Apple Account details, only that a purchase succeeded, so we can deliver the item.

Advertising

This version doesn't show ads from an ad network. If a future version does, this policy will be updated first, and the ad network will use your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.

Children

Screwhaus isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Keeping and deleting data

Shared play data is kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone. To stop sharing, turn it off in the shop under Privacy. To ask us to delete data already shared, open a support request with your iPhone model and roughly when you played, and we'll remove the matching data. Don't post anything personal in the request; it's public.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date below will change, and the app will ask again before sharing anything new.
