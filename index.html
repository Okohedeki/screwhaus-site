How it plays

Tap. Sort. Clear.

Every level is an object held together by colored screws. Find the ones you can reach and plan which color to free next.

1

Unscrew

Drag to spin the object and tap any screw you can reach.

2

Fill the box

Screws fly to the box of their color. A full box closes and slides away.

3

Clear it

Pull the last screw and the whole thing falls apart. Don't let the tray jam.

Support

Need a hand?

Answers to the common questions, and a way to reach us for everything else.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple. To restore them on a new phone, open the shop in the game and tap Restore purchases. For a refund, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

Progress

Your levels, coins and streak are saved on your iPhone. There's no account, so deleting the app deletes your progress.

Privacy

Sharing how you play is off until you say yes, and you can turn it off at any time in the shop under Privacy. Read the privacy policy.

Contact

Open a support request below. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and the level number if it's about a puzzle. Requests are public, so don't include anything personal.
